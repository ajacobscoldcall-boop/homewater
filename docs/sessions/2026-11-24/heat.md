1. Change the shape without waiting for the answer, as a reversible choice: heat stress on the return builds a lasting toll (a carried 'stress load') instead of killing at once in most cases; on the grounds the toll shortens the time before senescence and raises the chance of dying before spawning (a new death cause, for example prespawn), and crowding in warm water (the crowd's density) adds to it.
