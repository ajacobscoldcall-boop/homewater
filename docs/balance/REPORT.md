# Homewater balance report

Generated by `npm run balance` (scripts/balance/). Seed 20261119; params.json sha256 `75294b4a4fda`. 800 single lives per difficulty and skill, 150 spawning trials per role, and 50 lineages of up to 10 generations. 9,278 lives in all, about 641 hours of simulated play, in 922 s on 4 workers.

How to read it:
- **Per-fish death rate:** the share of attempts at a stage that end in death. A sibling resuming at a checkpoint is a new attempt.
- **Brood:** the first fish plus its siblings (siblings param + 1). The brood fails when the last fish dies.
- **Bots:** play through the public World API with Intent inputs only, reading what is on screen. Novice, average and skilled differ in reaction time, steering wobble, missed threats, attention lapses and habits; see `scripts/balance/skills.ts`.
- **Marks:** (easy) and **(hard)** compare against the design bands in `scripts/balance/targets.ts`, which are the simulator's proposal for the owner to confirm.
- **±** is a 95% interval.

## Summary

Hand-written after the realism, behaviour and feel merges (population, imprinting, water temperature, falls and fellow migrants, photo mode, animal behaviour, feel pass, shallows fix; seed 20261119). The previous summary was written after the egg-flood and smolt-fairness pass, so the "before" figures below are from that run. The bots are not people: read each number as "a player who plays like this profile", and judge feel in the browser.

### Where things stand

- **Field guide still works.** Lives completed: novice 82%, average 99%, skilled 99% (before: 82%, 95%, 100%).
- **Wild got harder, almost all of it in the home river.** Lives completed: novice 3%, average 41%, skilled 68% (before: 1%, 55%, 84%). Skilled Wild lines alive at generation 5: 18% (before 42%).
  - Home-river deaths per fish on Wild: average 69%, skilled 54% (before: 38%, 23%). On Field guide they barely moved: novice 60%, average 39%, skilled 18% (before 54%, 41%, 15%).
  - The cause is running out of fat (`exhaustion`), plus a little heat stress in warm years. A probe of 150 Wild return stages shows how it happens: fish reach the rapids with about 65% fat, spend about 30 s in the rapids and at the falls, and leave the falls with about 37%. About half of the exhaustion deaths happen above the falls, on the way to the grounds; the other half happen while still in the rapids or at the falls. The rapids and falls added time to the return without adding any fat.
  - Cutting all three return fat drains by 20% only brings Wild skilled home-river deaths from 63% to 43% in the probe, so the drain rate is not the only issue. Part of it is the bots' falls plan: they wait deep in the plunge pool for the lip bear to rest, burning fat. **Not retuned:** the brief was to leave Wild mostly alone, and whether the falls should cost this much on Wild is for the owner to judge in play.
- **Animal behaviour moved the other stages a little.**
  - Smolt run on Wild: skilled 30% (before 23%); the flocking school and char with personalities catch more good players.
  - Ocean on Field guide is a little easier: average 26%, skilled 17% (before 31%, 20%).
  - Spawning: bears take one fish per strike and often a neighbour, so bear deaths fell a lot (Wild novice spawning deaths 20%, before 58%). Field guide skilled spawning deaths rose to 16% (before 10%), almost all `noSpawn`: a skilled female that loses time to the patrolling bear sometimes runs out of season.
- **Egg and fry** are unchanged within noise.
- **Stage lengths:** the return now takes 65-75 s for a successful attempt (rapids, falls and migrants); a completed life takes about 7.5-8.6 minutes.

### What was applied since the last summary

- Population and fishery, imprinting and the home scent, water temperature and oxygen, the rapids and falls with fellow migrants (see the decision log in `CLAUDE.md`).
- Behaviour: flocking schools that flee strikes, predator personalities, a patrolling bear that takes one fish and leaves a carcass, `spawn.bearInterval` 10 -> 8.5.
- Feel: Field guide char warning 1.6× (was 1.3×).
- Shallows: the fish can no longer swim up onto a beach or bank above the surface (no balance effect measured).

### Still open

- **Wild home river (new, biggest):** see above. Options for the owner: a gentler fat cost on Wild in the rapids and at the falls, a little more fat at the river mouth on Wild, or leaving it as the hard part of Wild.
- **Fat in the home river on Field guide:** still the top cause of death upstream for novices (exhaustion 41%). Earlier note stands: it may be bot behaviour (waiting and wandering) rather than tuning; it is on the owner's checklist.
- **Wild smolt run** is still marked hard (89%, 54%, 30%), by choice.
- **Egg stage for average and skilled players** is below the band (0-1%); the visible brood loss carries the advisor's figure instead.
- **Brood size** is siblings + 1 (6 fish on Field guide, 4 on Wild), while DESIGN.md says about 3-5 lives. Left as is.

The tables below are generated and replace themselves on every run.
