# Homewater balance report

Generated by `npm run balance` (scripts/balance/). Seed 20261119; params.json sha256 `ed8eb5f1cbc4`. 800 single lives per difficulty and skill, 150 spawning trials per role, and 50 lineages of up to 10 generations. 9,560 lives in all, about 929 hours of simulated play, in 2173 s on 4 workers.

How to read it:
- **Per-fish death rate:** the share of attempts at a stage that end in death. A sibling resuming at a checkpoint is a new attempt.
- **Brood:** the first fish plus its siblings (siblings param + 1). The brood fails when the last fish dies.
- **Bots:** play through the public World API with Intent inputs only, reading what is on screen. Novice, average and skilled differ in reaction time, steering wobble, missed threats, attention lapses and habits; see `scripts/balance/skills.ts`.
- **Marks:** (easy) and **(hard)** compare against the design bands in `scripts/balance/targets.ts`, which are the simulator's proposal for the owner to confirm.
- **±** is a 95% interval.

## Summary

Hand-written after Track 2's second round and the falls rework (seed 20261119): egg threats, the ocean as a journey through the seasons, warm-year realism (a heat toll instead of sudden heat deaths), the changing river mode (off in this report: every line here is a healthy river), weather and sound (render only), and the falls rebuilt after a playtest. "Before" figures are from the last report.

### Length: towards the 20-30 minute first life

- **The report's single lives are later lives** (shorter by design). A completed later life now takes **11.8-13.0 minutes** of bot play (before: 9.2-10.8).
- **First lives, measured separately** (`scripts/balance/firstLife.ts`, 40 lives per row): about **15 minutes** of bot play on both difficulties (Field guide 15.1-15.4, Wild skilled 15.5). Per stage, a first life is about: egg 2 min, fry 2.8 min, smolt run 0.7-1.3 min, ocean 4-4.7 min, return 1.5-2 min, spawning 1.4 min.
- Bot time leaves out stage cards, cinematic shots, reading captions, the journal and the recap, and bots never hesitate. A person's first life is likely **about 18-22 minutes**: inside the owner's 20-30 at the low end. The play log will give the real figure.
- **Where the new time came from:** the ocean (about 3.5 min for a later life, 4-4.7 for a first, was 1.4) and the egg stage (2 min for a first life, about 55 s later, was 35 s).

### Where things stand

- **Field guide still works.** Lives completed: novice 81%, average 98%, skilled 100% (before 79/98/99%).
- **Wild got easier for average and skilled players.** Lives completed: novice 2%, average 70%, skilled 93% (before 1/54/81%); skilled Wild lines alive at generation 5: 74% (before 26%), at generation 10: 60% (before 4%). Skilled Wild is now marked easy. Causes: the falls rework (Wild falls deaths for novice and average bots 29% → 11% and 16% → 10%, and the side channel sooner), heat no longer kills on the spot, and the crowd on the grounds draws the bear. Worth a decision from the owner (below).
- **The falls** (probe in `docs/feel.md`): on Field guide every bot that reached the plunge pool cleared it by a leap within 3 tries, and falls deaths are 0-1%. On Wild, average bots clear within 3 tries 95% of the time (was 79%), novices 65% (was 43%).
- **New sections, deaths per fish:**
  - **Egg and alevin.** Field guide 6/0/0% (novice/average/skilled); Wild 69/7/0% (before 61/1/0%): the sculpin takes 18% of Wild novices. Wild novice is marked hard.
  - **Ocean years.** Field guide 38/20/4%, Wild 52/28/12% (before: four cells too hard). All in band now.
  - **Return home river.** Field guide 52/33/15%, Wild 84/44/28%: still marked hard on most cells (before 58/38/20%, 87/53/36%). Running out of fat is the top cause, then bears.
  - **Spawning.** Unchanged within noise; a few fish now die before spawning in warm years (`prespawn`, 0-1%).
- **Jacks** are 3-6% of males (creek base rate 4%; real run-wide 1-2%, and the captions say jacks are rare).

### Still open

- **Wild balance:** should skilled Wild stay this survivable (93% of lives completed, 60% of lines reach generation 10)? Options: a little more Wild fat drain on the return, or a stronger Wild ocean. Left as is until real play logs come in.
- **Home river** is still marked hard on Field guide; it may be bot behaviour (they swim hard and run out of fat). Real play logs will tell.
- **Wild smolt run** is still marked hard (88/47/28%), by choice.
- **Egg stage** for average and skilled players is under its band; the brood-mates in the nest carry the advisor's 10-20% instead (17-19% here).

The tables below are generated and replace themselves on every run.
