# Homewater balance report

Generated by `npm run balance` (scripts/balance/). Seed 20261119; params.json sha256 `54b49d77f9e5`. 800 single lives per difficulty and skill, 150 spawning trials per role, and 50 lineages of up to 10 generations. 9,431 lives in all, about 639 hours of simulated play, in 476 s on 4 workers.

How to read it:
- **Per-fish death rate:** the share of attempts at a stage that end in death. A sibling resuming at a checkpoint is a new attempt.
- **Brood:** the first fish plus its siblings (siblings param + 1). The brood fails when the last fish dies.
- **Bots:** play through the public World API with Intent inputs only, reading what is on screen. Novice, average and skilled differ in reaction time, steering wobble, missed threats, attention lapses and habits; see `scripts/balance/skills.ts`.
- **Marks:** (easy) and **(hard)** compare against the design bands in `scripts/balance/targets.ts`, which are the simulator's proposal for the owner to confirm.
- **±** is a 95% interval.

## Summary

Hand-written after the egg-flood and smolt-fairness pass (seed 20261119). The bots are not people: read each number as "a player who plays like this profile", and judge feel in the browser.

### Where things stand

- **Field guide works, and newer players now get further.** Lives completed: novice 82%, average 95%, skilled 100% (before this pass: 67%, 94%, 99%).
- **Wild is hard but survivable with skill.** Lives completed: novice 1%, average 55%, skilled 84% (before: 1%, 49%, 84%). 42% of skilled Wild lines are alive at generation 5. Novices still cannot keep a Wild line going; the novice band for Wild is probably what should change, not the game.
- **The egg stage now has a flood.** Deaths per fish: Field guide novice 8%, average and skilled 0%; Wild novice 61%, average 1%, skilled 0% (before: 0-6% everywhere). The flood mostly tests turning quickly to sudden swings, so keyboard play (the novice profile) feels it far more than mouse play. The brood-mates drawn in the nest emerge at 18-21%, against the advisor's 10-20%: the loss is seen, not played.
- **The smolt run is fairer on Field guide.** Deaths per fish: novice 30%, average 8%, skilled 3% (before: 64%, 26%, 4%), all inside the bands. Seals and birds still kill most, but a strike among school-mates usually takes one of them, and a near miss gives a moment's grace. Wild moved a little: novice 85%, average 59%, skilled 23% (before: 91%, 65%, 24%).
- **Stage lengths:** the egg stage is unchanged at about 35-37 s with the flood inside it; a completed life takes about 7.5-8.5 minutes.

### What was applied

- Egg: a flood once per egg stage (`alevin.flood*`), with a longer warning and weaker swings on Field guide, a shorter warning and harder scour on Wild; twelve brood-mates (`alevin.broodCount`), most washed out (`alevin.broodFloodLoss` × the year's flow).
- Smolt: `smolt.strikeDilution` (Field guide 0.8, Wild 0.2), `smolt.nearMissGrace` (Field guide 3 s, Wild 0.75 s), Field guide bird and seal warnings 1.5× (were 1.3×).
- Bots: the egg bot notices the flood (unless it misses the warning) and stops any early rise until it passes.

### Still open

- **Fat in the home river.** Running out of fat is still the top cause of death upstream on Field guide. It may be bot behaviour (waiting and wandering) rather than tuning; it is on the owner's checklist.
- **Wild smolt run** is still marked hard for novice and average players (85% and 59%), by choice: the brief was to leave Wild mostly alone.
- **Egg stage for average and skilled players** is below the band (0-1%). The flood could be harsher, but only by making it much worse for keyboard players; the visible brood loss carries the advisor's figure instead. Judge in play.
- **Brood size** is siblings + 1 (6 fish on Field guide, 4 on Wild), while DESIGN.md says about 3-5 lives. Left as is.
