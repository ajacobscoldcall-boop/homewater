# Homewater balance report

Generated by `npm run balance` (scripts/balance/). Seed 20261119; params.json sha256 `e5d54c01da54`. 800 single lives per difficulty and skill, 150 spawning trials per role, and 50 lineages of up to 10 generations. 9,325 lives in all, about 646 hours of simulated play, in 957 s on 4 workers.

How to read it:
- **Per-fish death rate:** the share of attempts at a stage that end in death. A sibling resuming at a checkpoint is a new attempt.
- **Brood:** the first fish plus its siblings (siblings param + 1). The brood fails when the last fish dies.
- **Bots:** play through the public World API with Intent inputs only, reading what is on screen. Novice, average and skilled differ in reaction time, steering wobble, missed threats, attention lapses and habits; see `scripts/balance/skills.ts`.
- **Marks:** (easy) and **(hard)** compare against the design bands in `scripts/balance/targets.ts`, which are the simulator's proposal for the owner to confirm.
- **±** is a 95% interval.

## Summary

Hand-written after the realism, behaviour and feel merges and the gentler falls (seed 20261119). "Before" figures are from the run after the egg-flood and smolt-fairness pass, unless marked "first run": that was the run right after the merges, before the falls were made gentler. The bots are not people: read each number as "a player who plays like this profile", and judge feel in the browser.

### Where things stand

- **Field guide still works.** Lives completed: novice 83%, average 98%, skilled 99% (before: 82%, 95%, 100%).
- **Wild is harder than before the realism work, but survivable with skill.** Lives completed: novice 2%, average 54%, skilled 76% (before: 1%, 55%, 84%; first run: 3%, 41%, 68%). Skilled Wild lines alive at generation 5: 28% (before 42%, first run 18%).
- **The home river is where Wild got harder, and the falls were most of it.**
  - The first run after the merges showed skilled Wild bots dying in the home river 54% of the time (23% before).
  - A probe of the Wild return found the cause. Fish reached the rapids with about 65% fat and spent about 30 s in the rapids and at the falls. They left the falls with about 37% and ran out on the way to the grounds. The falls had added hard swimming with no fat to pay for it.
  - The owner left the call to us. Swimming and bursting in the falls zone now cost half their usual fat (`return.fallsEffortFat` 0.5, both difficulties). On Wild the base and swimming burn on the whole return is 0.8×.
  - Home-river deaths per fish on Wild are now average 52% and skilled 39% (first run 69% and 54%). On Field guide they are novice 58%, average 37% and skilled 16% (before 54%, 41% and 15%).
  - Going further would mean tuning to the bots' falls plan (waiting deep in the pool for the lip bear), so the rest is left for the owner to judge in play (checklist item 87).
- **Animal behaviour moved the other stages a little.**
  - Smolt run on Wild: skilled 30% (before 23%); the flocking school and char with personalities catch more good players.
  - Ocean on Field guide is a little easier: average 26%, skilled 17% (before 31%, 20%).
  - Spawning: bears take one fish per strike and often a neighbour, so bear deaths fell (Wild average spawning deaths 12%, before 17%). Field guide skilled spawning deaths rose to 16% (before 10%), almost all `noSpawn`: a skilled female that loses time to the patrolling bear sometimes runs out of season. More Wild novices now reach the grounds, and a quarter of them run out of season there.
- **Egg and fry** are unchanged within noise.
- **Stage lengths:** a successful return takes 65-75 s (rapids, falls and migrants); a completed life takes about 7.5-8.7 minutes.

### What was applied since the last summary

- Population and fishery, imprinting and the home scent, water temperature and oxygen, the rapids and falls with fellow migrants (see the decision log in `CLAUDE.md`).
- Behaviour: flocking schools that flee strikes, predator personalities, a patrolling bear that takes one fish and leaves a carcass, `spawn.bearInterval` 10 -> 8.5.
- Feel: Field guide char warning 1.6× (was 1.3×).
- Shallows: the fish can no longer swim up onto a beach or bank above the surface.
- Gentler falls: `return.fallsEffortFat` 0.5; Wild `return.fatDrainBase` and `return.fatDrainSwim` 0.8×.

### Still open

- **Home river on Field guide:** still marked hard for all three profiles (58%, 37%, 16%); running out of fat is the top cause for novices (37%). It may be bot behaviour (waiting and wandering) rather than tuning; it is on the owner's checklist.
- **Wild smolt run** is still marked hard (89%, 54%, 30%), by choice.
- **Egg stage for average and skilled players** is below the band (0-1%); the visible brood loss carries the advisor's figure instead.
- **Brood size** is siblings + 1 (6 fish on Field guide, 4 on Wild), while DESIGN.md says about 3-5 lives. Left as is.

The tables below are generated and replace themselves on every run.
