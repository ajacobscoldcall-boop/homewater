# Homewater balance report

Generated by `npm run balance` (scripts/balance/). Seed 20261119; params.json sha256 `dac4e6b216f6`. 800 single lives per difficulty and skill, 150 spawning trials per role, and 50 lineages of up to 10 generations. 9,400 lives in all, about 637 hours of simulated play, in 769 s on 4 workers.

How to read it:
- **Per-fish death rate:** the share of attempts at a stage that end in death. A sibling resuming at a checkpoint is a new attempt.
- **Brood:** the first fish plus its siblings (siblings param + 1). The brood fails when the last fish dies.
- **Bots:** play through the public World API with Intent inputs only, reading what is on screen. Novice, average and skilled differ in reaction time, steering wobble, missed threats, attention lapses and habits; see `scripts/balance/skills.ts`.
- **Marks:** (easy) and **(hard)** compare against the design bands in `scripts/balance/targets.ts`, which are the simulator's proposal for the owner to confirm.
- **±** is a 95% interval.

## Summary

Hand-written after the balance pass (seed 20261119). The bots are not people: read each number as "a player who plays like this profile", and judge feel in the browser.

### Where things stand

- **Field guide works.** Lives completed: novice 67%, average 94%, skilled 99% (before the pass: 21%, 87%, 99%).
- **Wild is hard but survivable with skill.** Lives completed: novice 1%, average 49%, skilled 84% (before: 0%, 3%, 12%). 45% of skilled Wild lines are still alive at generation 5. Novices still cannot keep a Wild line going; the novice band for Wild is probably what should change, not the game.
- **Jacks now match the advisor's rates more closely.** 5-6% of maturation decisions come out jack (creek base rate 4%), down from 9-16%. The fix centred the growth effect on typical growth (`genetics.jackGrowthReference`).
- **Stage lengths:** a completed life takes about 7.5-8.5 minutes, with the rebuilt fry (about 85 s) and ocean (about 85 s).

### What was applied

- Smolt run: stronger school shield (FG multiplier 1.3, Wild 0.85), slower seals and mergansers, smaller seal catch radius; the Wild bird-interval multiplier was dropped.
- Ocean: the extra-years roll keeps the advisor's 0.5 base, with multipliers FG 1.8 and Wild 1.4; the new ocean seal is no faster on Wild.
- Return: less fat drain, no Wild fat multiplier, weaker current, smaller bear swipe, `return.bearRest` (5 s, was a hard-coded 3 s), a third net gap on Field guide, and a fat floor for a sibling taking over at the river mouth.
- Alevin on Wild: harsher surges (drift multiplier 1.6) with shorter warnings (0.65).
- Code: char and mergansers no longer switch to the player when their school-mate is taken; fry food no longer spawns inside the weed shelf; the dead alevin exposure params were removed; the bots now dodge the ocean seal.
- Not applied: faster fry trout and more fry trout on Wild (the rebuilt fry already kills 1-42% of fish), and a lower creek jack rate (4% is the advisor's figure).

### Still open

- **Fat in the home river.** Running out of fat is still the top cause of death upstream (19-44% of attempts on Field guide). Lowering the drain, the burst cost or the bear rest barely changed it, so it may be bot behaviour (waiting and wandering) rather than tuning. It is on the owner's checklist to judge in play.
- **The smolt run** is still the hardest stage for novice and average players on Field guide (64% and 26% deaths per fish).
- **Egg stage** kills almost nobody on Field guide, and nothing carries the advisor's 80-90% egg-to-fry loss off screen. DESIGN.md plans a flood or sibling-loss beat for this.
- **Brood size** is siblings + 1 (6 fish on Field guide, 4 on Wild), while DESIGN.md says about 3-5 lives. Left as is: smaller broods would make every number above harder.

The tables below are generated and replace themselves on every run.
