# Homewater balance report

Generated by `npm run balance` (scripts/balance/). Seed 20261119; params.json sha256 `77468dfc9ec3`. 800 single lives per difficulty and skill, 150 spawning trials per role, and 50 lineages of up to 10 generations. 9,330 lives in all, about 783 hours of simulated play, in 1642 s on 4 workers.

How to read it:
- **Per-fish death rate:** the share of attempts at a stage that end in death. A sibling resuming at a checkpoint is a new attempt.
- **Brood:** the first fish plus its siblings (siblings param + 1). The brood fails when the last fish dies.
- **Bots:** play through the public World API with Intent inputs only, reading what is on screen. Novice, average and skilled differ in reaction time, steering wobble, missed threats, attention lapses and habits; see `scripts/balance/skills.ts`.
- **Marks:** (easy) and **(hard)** compare against the design bands in `scripts/balance/targets.ts`, which are the simulator's proposal for the owner to confirm.
- **±** is a 95% interval.

## Summary

Hand-written after Track 2's first round (seed 20261119): fry lake days and mergansers, the estuary at the end of the smolt run, home through the nursery lake, and crowded spawning grounds. "Before" figures are from the run after the gentler falls. The bots are not people: read each number as "a player who plays like this profile", and judge feel in the browser.

### Length: towards the 20-30 minute first life

- **A completed life now takes about 9.6-10.8 minutes of play** (before: 7.5-8.7). This is bot play time only: it leaves out stage cards, cinematic shots, reading captions and the recap, which add a few minutes for a person.
- **Where the time went:**
  - The fry stage is now about 135 s (was about 84) with two lake days.
  - The smolt run with the estuary is 56-79 s (was about 41).
  - The return with the lake crossing is 98-125 s (was 65-75).
  - Egg, ocean and spawning are unchanged in length.
- **Still short of the owner's 20-30 minutes.** The ocean is the most compressed stage (about 85 s for 2-3 years), and the remaining Track 2 work (the ocean as a journey, egg threats) is where most of the extra length should come from.

### Where things stand

- **Field guide still works.** Lives completed: novice 79%, average 98%, skilled 99% (before: 83%, 98%, 99%).
- **Wild is about where it was, skilled a little easier.** Lives completed: novice 1%, average 54%, skilled 81% (before: 2%, 54%, 76%). Skilled Wild lines alive at generation 5: 26% (before 28%).
- **New sections, deaths per fish:**
  - **Fry (lake days and mergansers).** Field guide 34/13/7% for novice, average and skilled (before 20/7/0%); Wild 53/22/11% (before 42/14/12%). Mergansers take 1-12%; char are still the main killer. All in band.
  - **Smolt run with the estuary.** Field guide 36/8/2% (before 24/8/1%), within band; Wild 90/48/27% (before 89/54/30%), still marked hard as before. Gulls at the river mouth now kill some novices (8% on Field guide).
  - **Across the lake (new row).** Field guide 18/6/5%; Wild novice 47% (bears at the stream mouths), average 16%, skilled 5%. All in band.
  - **Spawning in the crowd.** Field guide 12/4/6% (before 24/7/16%); Wild 22/7/7% (before 38/12/16%). A bear's strike now usually takes a crowd fish, so spawning is safer for the player; Wild average is now just under its band. The danger moved to the eggs: char and gulls take some of an uncovered clutch.
- **Home river** is unchanged within noise (Field guide 58/38/20%, Wild 87/53/36%), still marked hard, as discussed in the last summary.

### What was applied since the last summary

- Fry: lake days (deep by day, up at dusk and dawn), char that see by the light, mergansers from above (`fry.*`).
- Smolt run: the estuary at first light, salinity and readiness for salt water, gulls at the mouth (`estuary.*`).
- Return: the crossing of the nursery lake, waiting for spawning colours, a warm top layer in warm years, bears at the stream mouths (`return.lake.*`; checkpoint marker 3).
- Spawning: a crowd that scales with the run, egg thieves (char and gulls), `spawn.crowd.bearAttention` 0.25, Wild `spawn.bearInterval` 0.85×.

### Still open

- **Length:** about 10 minutes of bot play against the owner's 20-30. The ocean journey and the egg stage's threats are next.
- **Home river on both difficulties** is still marked hard; running out of fat is the top cause. It may be bot behaviour; real play logs will tell.
- **Wild smolt run** is still marked hard (90/48/27%), by choice.
- **Spawning on Wild for average players** is now slightly easy (7%, band 10-45%): the crowd shields the player from bears. Worth watching in real play before tuning back.
- **Egg stage for average and skilled players** is below the band (0-1%); the visible brood loss carries the advisor's figure instead.
