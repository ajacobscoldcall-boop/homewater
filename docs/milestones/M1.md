# Milestone 1: a whole life in grey boxes

**Updated after milestone 1:** the egg stage and the smolt run have been redesigned from the advisor's answers, the water has a proper look, and threats are marked on screen. Items 2 and 4 below describe the new versions.

What this milestone is for: proving the full loop works and finding out whether swimming feels right. The fish, animals and places are now built in code. Sound is generated in the browser and starts on your first click. Judge the feel and the flow, not the looks.

## How to play

Open the game link. Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a desktop or laptop.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Click the game | Capture the mouse for steering (Esc releases it and pauses) |
| Mouse or A / D | Steer |
| W | Swim forward |
| S | Ease off and hold position against the current |
| Space / C | Rise / dive (in the egg stage, Space wriggles) |
| Shift | Burst: fast, but burns energy (and fat on the return) |
| E | Act: dig the redd, or dart in as a jack |
| V | Switch between third- and first-person |
| H | Show details: energy, fat, oxygen, growth, depth |
| M | Sound on or off (volume is on the title screen and in the pause menu) |

A whole life takes about 8 to 10 minutes if nothing goes wrong.

## Try these

1. [ ] Start a **Quick start** female on **Field guide**. Play one whole life to the end and pick an offspring.
2. [ ] **Egg and alevin:** after hatching, turn to face into the current when the drifting flecks swing round (A/D or mouse). Stay low (C) during surges. When the yolk is nearly gone, hold Space to swim up toward the light. Does it make sense without the captions?
3. [ ] **Fry (a year in the lake, as short scenes with time skips):** at the weed edge, wait for the rings and dart into the weeds. At dusk, feed up near the surface but keep the weeds close. Under the winter ice, rest (let go of W) and take only the food that drifts close. In spring, follow the current to the outlet to leave, or wait in the weeds to stay a second year. Is each scene clear without the captions? Does the time skip read as time passing? Can you see the ice (try V for first person and look up)?
4. [ ] **Smolt gauntlet:** stay with the school. Burst away from char at the lake outlet. Dive under bird shadows. Swerve and burst past a merganser's splash. At the river mouth, swim across a seal's path. Does it feel like a gauntlet? Is it fair?
5. [ ] **Ocean (years at sea, as short scenes):** feed in the krill swarm (pink specks, with a few small fish and squid). When the orca and later the salmon shark charge, swim across their path. When a seal appears near the surface, turn hard as it lunges. In a crowded pink-salmon year, race the pinks to the food. Watch the first-autumn scene at the end. Is the seal easy enough to spot during its warning?
6. [ ] **Return (navigation):** follow the faint blue shimmer of the magnetic track across open water. If it fades, sweep side to side to find it again. Thread a gap in the nets, then choose a river mouth. If a river doesn't smell like home, back out. Upstream, wait in a pool for a bear's swipe, then cross the shallows. At the fork, test a branch and back out if the scent fades. Did you ever get lost? Did the fat bar run low? 7. [ ] **Spawn (female):** find the lighter gravel, hold E to dig, and charge rival females with Shift.

### Feel pass

The camera, steering and warnings were tuned without anyone playing by hand. Only you can judge these. (Notes: `docs/feel.md`. Pictures: `docs/gallery/feel-*.png`.)

77. [ ] **Camera against banks and rocks:** swim hard along the banks in each stage, into the lake's beach, under the river's overhangs and between the rocks on the spawning grounds. The camera should never go inside the ground, a rock or a log, and never above the water. When something is in the way it moves in closer, rises, or swings to one side. Does it ever jump or snap in a way that bothers you? Does it ever lose sight of your fish? (Known gap: in the lake you can swim right up onto the beach, out of the water. The camera can't follow you there.)
78. [ ] **Camera on turns:** turn hard left and right. The camera follows a moment behind, so the fish swings across the screen and you can see which way it is heading. Too much lag, too little, or about right? Is the fish in a comfortable place on screen, not hidden behind captions? (`docs/gallery/feel-camera-return-12.png`)
79. [ ] **Fish banking:** when your fish turns it should lean into the turn, like a bike. Does it look natural, too much, or too little?
80. [ ] **Steering keys:** tap A or D: the fish should make a small correction. Hold: it builds up to a full turn in a fraction of a second. Does it feel smooth, or now sluggish? With a gamepad, is the stick fine enough near the centre?
81. [ ] **Mouse:** on the Controls page there is now **Mouse sensitivity** (0.5× to 3×) and **Mouse up and down** (normal or inverted) (`docs/gallery/feel-controls-mouse.png`). What setting do you end up using? Should the default be different?
