# Milestone 1: a whole life in grey boxes

**Updated after milestone 1:** the egg stage and the smolt run have been redesigned from the advisor's answers, the water has a proper look, and threats are marked on screen. Items 2 and 4 below describe the new versions.

What this milestone is for: proving the full loop works and finding out whether swimming feels right. The fish, animals and places are now built in code. Sound is generated in the browser and starts on your first click. Judge the feel and the flow, not the looks.

## How to play

Open the game link. Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a desktop or laptop.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Click the game | Capture the mouse for steering (Esc releases it and pauses) |
| Mouse or A / D | Steer |
| W | Swim forward |
| S | Ease off and hold position against the current |
| Space / C | Rise / dive (in the egg stage, Space wriggles) |
| Shift | Burst: fast, but burns energy (and fat on the return) |
| E | Act: dig the redd, or dart in as a jack |
| V | Switch between third- and first-person |
| H | Show details: energy, fat, oxygen, growth, depth |
| M | Sound on or off (volume is on the title screen and in the pause menu) |

A whole life takes about 8 to 10 minutes if nothing goes wrong.

## Try these

Tick each one, and note anything that feels wrong, confusing or dull.

1. [ ] Start a **Quick start** female on **Field guide**. Play one whole life to the end and pick an offspring.
2. [ ] **Egg and alevin:** after hatching, turn to face into the current when the drifting flecks swing round (A/D or mouse). Stay low (C) during surges. When the yolk is nearly gone, hold Space to swim up toward the light. Does it make sense without the captions?
3. [ ] **Fry (a year in the lake, as short scenes with time skips):** at the weed edge, wait for the rings and dart into the weeds. At dusk, feed up near the surface but keep the weeds close. Under the winter ice, rest (let go of W) and take only the food that drifts close. In spring, follow the current to the outlet to leave, or wait in the weeds to stay a second year. Is each scene clear without the captions? Does the time skip read as time passing? Can you see the ice (try V for first person and look up)?
4. [ ] **Smolt gauntlet:** stay with the school. Burst away from char at the lake outlet. Dive under bird shadows. Swerve and burst past a merganser's splash. At the river mouth, swim across a seal's path. Does it feel like a gauntlet? Is it fair?
5. [ ] **Ocean (years at sea, as short scenes):** feed in the krill swarm (pink specks, with a few small fish and squid). When the orca and later the salmon shark charge, swim across their path. When a seal appears near the surface, turn hard as it lunges. In a crowded pink-salmon year, race the pinks to the food. Watch the first-autumn scene at the end. Is the seal easy enough to spot during its warning?
6. [ ] **Return (navigation):** follow the faint blue shimmer of the magnetic track across open water. If it fades, sweep side to side to find it again. Thread a gap in the nets, then choose a river mouth. If a river doesn't smell like home, back out. Upstream, wait in a pool for a bear's swipe, then cross the shallows. At the fork, test a branch and back out if the scent fades. Did you ever get lost? Did the fat bar run low? (Test bots often run out of fat in the home river, more than intended; tell me whether you do.)
7. [ ] **Spawn (female):** find the lighter gravel, hold E to dig, and charge rival females with Shift.
8. [ ] Start a new lineage with **Start as a jack**. In the spawning stage, settle into a hiding spot under the bank (the rings: green is yours, red is another jack's). Charge a jack out with Shift if you need to. When the screen flashes green (the big males are fighting), dart in next to the female and press E.
9. [ ] Play a **male** that becomes a large male. Stay near the female and ram rivals side-on, before they reach her. Watch for bears: they go for big males first.
10. [ ] Die on purpose. Check that a sibling takes over, and that the siblings counter (◆) goes down.
11. [ ] Reload the page partway through a life. Check that **Continue** resumes it.
12. [ ] Press **V** to try first-person, and **H** for the detailed bars.
13. [ ] Try **Custom** start and the **Wild** difficulty.
14. [ ] Start lineages in each home habitat: **Small creek**, **River** and **Lake beach**. Does each feel different on the spawning grounds?
15. [ ] Play a few generations and read the year line on the first stage card and in the recap. Do different years seem to favour different fish?
16. [ ] **Sound:** play a few stages with sound on. Then press **M** to mute and play on. Does each place sound different? Do the warning sounds (the low pulse before an orca, the splash of a bird or bear) help you react, or are they distracting? Is anything too loud, too quiet or annoying on repeat?
17. [ ] **Sound settings:** change the volume on the title screen and in the pause menu (Esc). Reload the page and check the setting is kept. Pausing should fade the sound down.
18. [ ] On a **Field guide** first life, follow the step-by-step prompts at the top left. Does each one tick over when you do the thing? Is any step confusing or stuck? Try **Skip guidance** (G) once, and check a second life has no prompts.
19. [ ] Open the **Field journal** from the title screen, and with **J** while paused. Do new entries pop up as you meet predators, die, or reach a stage? Read the **Facts to remember** page.

20. [ ] **Animals:** open the game with `?gallery=1` at the end of the address to see every fish and animal side by side. Then, in play, watch how the salmon's fins and tail move, the bear's swipe on the spawning grounds, a gull's plunge in the smolt run, and the merganser's underwater chase. Does anything look wrong or move oddly?
21. [ ] **Spawning as a female:** dig the redd, then wait over it. Do the arriving males, and the fight between two of them, read clearly? Cover the eggs with E, then guard. Does the redd change colour when covered? Does the slow loss of strength (the energy bar can't refill past it) feel like a natural end, or a timer? Is about two minutes the right length?
22. [ ] **Spawning as a large male:** after spawning, a second female arrives along the bed. Is the choice to go to her or stay clear? Does leaving feel risky enough (strength drops, bears come more often)?
23. [ ] **Spawning as a jack:** play a few jack lives in different years. Do the captions about many or few jacks this year make sense? Does the waiting in cover get boring before your strength runs out?
24. [ ] **Family tree:** play two or three generations. On the recap, does the drawn tree read clearly: who spawned (red dot), who died (hollow), the siblings lost (small grey dots) and the offspring you didn't pick (faint names)? Hover the dots for details.
25. [ ] **Records and score:** read the lineage score line and the records on the recap. Is the one-line explanation of the score clear? Open **Lineages and records** from the title screen and click a line to see its tree, trait chart and years.
26. [ ] **Extinction:** start a **Wild** lineage and let a whole brood die (or use `?debug=1` and **Kill fish**). Does the ending screen feel like a proper end for the line? Is the trait chart readable? Try **A stray from this river**.
27. [ ] **Export and import:** on the title screen, export your lineage file, start a new lineage, then import the file. Does your old line come back, with the new one kept on the Lineages page?
28. [ ] **Controls:** open **Controls** from the title screen (and from the pause menu). Click the key next to **Swim forward**, press a different key, and play a little. Do the hint at the bottom right, the pause menu, the field guide steps and the captions (for example the one at the start of the lake) all name your new key? Reload and check it is kept. Try **Reset to defaults**.
29. [ ] **Hold or toggle:** on the Controls page set **Swim forward** and **Burst** to **Toggle**. Does press-once-to-swim feel comfortable on a long swim like the return? Does ease off (S) stop it as you'd expect?
30. [ ] **Gamepad** (if you have one): plug it in, press a button, and check the Controls page names it. Play the lake and the smolt run with it: left stick steers, right trigger swims, A bursts, bumpers rise and dive, X acts, Start pauses. Does steering feel too twitchy or too slow?
31. [ ] **Readability:** open **Readability**. Try the three caption sizes and the background slider. Turn on **High contrast** and **Colour-blind-safe colours**, then play the smolt run: are the markers on the char and birds easier to spot? Turn on **Reduced motion** and check the screen-edge flash is softer. Set **Game speed** to 0.75× and say whether the slower game helps or just drags.
32. [ ] **Ocean food:** in the ocean years, does the food patch read as a swarm of krill (small pink, see-through shrimp-like animals), with a few small fish and the odd small squid among them? Do they drift and twitch naturally, and is the swarm easy to spot from a distance? (`?gallery=krill` and `?gallery=squid` show them up close.)
33. [ ] **Title screen:** open **How to play** and **About**. Is anything missing, wrong or too long? Does **Back** return to the title?
34. [ ] **Sharing:** watch `docs/media/homewater-clip.webm` and look at the stills in `docs/screenshots/`. Paste the site link into a chat app: does a preview card with a picture appear? Read the new `README.md`.

35. [ ] **Gore (Unflinching, the default):** play a life and watch your fish. Near misses leave marks: claw rakes from a bear that just missed, a scrape from a char or a bird, tooth rakes from an orca or a seal, a bite arc after a fight on the grounds, a torn mouth after escaping a hook, a rubbed band behind the head after squeezing through a net. Raw wounds turn pink, then pale, and fade a lot across the years at sea. Do the marks read as wounds? Are they too much, or too little?
36. [ ] **Worn out on the grounds:** as spawning goes on, your fish (and the others) should darken, grow pale fungus patches on the head, tail and fins, and fray at the fin edges. Does the change show, and does it feel right for the end of a life?
37. [ ] **A death by a predator:** let a bear or an orca take you. On Unflinching there is a short beat (the strike, blood and scales in the water, the predator turning away with the fish) before the death screen; any key skips it. Is it too long, too short, too graphic, or not clear enough? Then switch the title screen's **Gore** setting to **Documentary** and die again: it should fade to black on the strike.
38. [ ] **After spawning:** a short series of fades plays: the spent fish, its death on the gravel, the carcass close up (Unflinching only), the forest, winter into spring, the eggs. Any key or click skips it. Does it feel like a fitting end, or does it drag? Try it in Documentary too.

39. [ ] **Graphics:** on the title screen (or in the pause menu, Esc) try each **Graphics** setting: **Low**, **Medium**, **High** and **Auto**. Add `?debug=1` to the address and note the fps in the panel for each, in the lake (fry) and the ocean. On **Low**, can you still see every threat in time (the char, orca, seals, bears)? Does **High** look the same as before? With **Auto**, play a minute and say which level the panel shows (for example "Auto: Medium").
40. [ ] **Places:** every stage now has its own scenery: cobble in the redd, a weedy lake shelf with a drop-off and sunken logs, a night river with roots and fallen trees, open ocean, a rocky coast with three river mouths and riffles, and three different spawning grounds (pictures in `docs/gallery/env-*.png`). As you play, ask:
    - Does each place feel like somewhere real?
    - Can you still pick out what matters at a glance: plankton, gravel patches, nets, lures, the hiding-spot rings, the magnetic track, and the predators?
    - Do the river banks look like where the fish actually stops?
    - Is the weed bed in the lake clear as "safe here"?
    - Does anything in the scenery look wrong or move oddly?
    - Does the game still run smoothly on your computer? Look at frames per second with `?debug=1`.

41. [ ] **Cinematic moments:** play a life with **Readability → Cinematic moments** on (the default). A short framed shot (2-3 seconds, with black bars top and bottom) should play when the egg hatches (low among the cobbles), when the lake first opens up, as the smolts leave the lake at night (wide, from the side), on reaching the sea (from below, the fish against the bright surface), at the autumn maturation reveal (a slow half-circle round the fish), at the river mouth that smells like home (rising behind the fish, looking upriver), and at the spawning act (side-on to the pair over the redd). Do they add something, or interrupt? Too long or too short? Is any badly framed (fish hidden, wall in the way)? The game keeps running and the controls keep working during a shot: press any key or click to cut back early. Was that the right choice, or would you rather the game paused for them?
42. [ ] **Cinematics and safety:** a shot should never start while something is attacking or a red marker is showing, and should cut straight back if an attack starts. Did you ever lose sight of a threat because of one? Turn on **Reduced motion**: the camera should cut to a still shot and back instead of moving. Turn **Cinematic moments** off and check none play. Each moment plays only once per fish, even after a reload; a sibling sees them again.

43. [ ] **Brown bear:** open `?gallery=bear`, `?gallery=bearStrike` and `?gallery=bearCatch` (side by side in `docs/gallery/models-bear.png` and `models-bear-strike.png`). Does the bear look shaggy rather than spiky? Do the legs show elbows and wrists, and do the big paws and long front claws read? On the spawning grounds, watch a bear strike from under the water: do its head and forepaws plunge in towards its shadow?
44. [ ] **Taken by a bear:** let a bear catch you (Unflinching). Is your fish visible in its jaws under the water for the whole beat (`docs/gallery/models-bear-death-beat.png`)? Too much, or not clear enough?
45. [ ] **Harbor seal:** `?gallery=seal`. Does it read as a seal now: round head, short muzzle, whiskers, a neck, flippers? In the ocean, does a lunging seal look right?
46. [ ] **The carcass:** after spawning (Unflinching), does the close-up read as a dead, spawned-out sockeye lying on its side among stones (dull dark red going grey-brown, pale eroded patches, ragged fins, a clouded eye), not a koi (`docs/gallery/models-carcass.png`)?
47. [ ] **Pink salmon:** in a pink year at sea, can you tell the pinks from your own fish by their dark oval spots on the back and tail (`?gallery=pink`)?
48. [ ] **Small fish and the merganser:** do the small fish in the krill swarm look like slim forage fish rather than tiny salmon (`?gallery=herring`)? Does the female merganser's neck look smooth, and do the wing feathers read (`?gallery=mergFemale`)?

49. [ ] **Bear strike, from under the water:** on the spawning grounds (creek, river and lake beach) and at the riffles on the way home, wait near the bank for a bear. It should stay on the bank or in the shallows with its feet on the ground, then lunge so only its head and forelegs come down through the surface towards the fish, in a burst of white bubbles. Its body stays above the water, so from below you mostly see the legs and head (`docs/gallery/above-bear-strike-before-after.png`, `above-riffle-before-after.png`, `above-bear-beach.png`). Does it read as a bear reaching in from the bank? Is the splash too much or too little?
50. [ ] **Bears and the middle of the creek:** a bear can no longer reach the middle of the creek on screen, but it can still catch you there (the rules are unchanged). If it does, your fish is hauled to the bank in its jaws and the camera closes in under the water (`docs/gallery/above-bear-death-beat.png`). Does that feel fair, or should bears only catch fish near the banks? That would change the rules, so it is your call.
51. [ ] **Looking up:** swim up and look at the surface (V for first person helps). Inside the bright circle overhead you should see the sky and, near the banks, a dark tree line; outside it the surface is a darker mirror. Check day (lake, river, spawning grounds), dusk and the winter ice (lake), night with stars (the smolt run), and open sky only at sea (`docs/gallery/above-sky-creek.png`, `above-sky-beach.png`, `above-sky-lake.png`, `above-night-before-after.png`). Does the surface now match the colour of the water around it, with no darker band? At **Low** graphics you should see the sky only (`above-sky-low.png`). Any drop in frame rate at High?

52. [ ] **The flood (egg stage):** about a third of the way through the yolk, the water darkens and clouds with silt and a caption warns of a flood; a few seconds later the flecks race and the flow swings suddenly from side to side for about 7 seconds (`docs/gallery/flood-brood.png`, `flood-warning.png`, `flood-flood.png`). Is the warning clear and long enough to get ready? Does riding it out (deep in the gravel, keep turning to face upstream) feel tense but fair? Too long or too short? On **Wild** the warning is shorter and the swings and scour are harder: is that still fair?
53. [ ] **Brood-mates:** about a dozen alevin hatch around yours. The flood tears most of them out and they tumble away; the card afterwards says how many were lost (`docs/gallery/flood-over.png`). Do you see it happen? Does it get across that only about 10-20% of eggs make it, without feeling cruel or preachy? Can you always tell which alevin is yours?
54. [ ] **Smolt run on Field guide:** it should be noticeably fairer. Staying in the thick of the school now saves you more often: a bird or seal that reaches you among school-mates usually takes one of them instead, and after a close call ("Close. Back into the thick of the school...") predators go for other fish for a moment. Shadows and seal shapes also warn a little longer. Is it still exciting, or now too easy? On **Wild** it is only slightly kinder.

## Questions for you

- **Swimming:** does it feel like swimming? Is it too fast, too slow, too floaty or too twitchy?
- **Effort:** which stage felt like a chore, and which was most fun?
- **Captions:** were any warnings or captions unclear?
- **Frame rate:** add `?debug=1` to the address. The panel shows frames per second (fps) and the graphics level. What does it read on your computer, at each graphics setting?

## Known gaps (planned for later milestones)

- **Visuals:** everything is built in code (see `?gallery=1` and `docs/gallery/`). Above the water there is only a sky and a tree-line silhouette seen through the surface; nothing above the water is modelled. The bear's fur is cards and vertex colour, not real fur.
- **Audio:** sound and music are generated placeholders. A composer's recordings can replace the music later (see `docs/audio.md`).
- **Narration:** no lines are recorded yet; captions show as text (see `docs/narration/`).
- **Advisor review:** a third round of questions and the screenshot pack are with the advisor; open questions are listed in `docs/ADVISOR_NOTES.md`.
